Info

A Minecraft-flavoured pixel-perfect generator for rounded shapes.

1. What it is

Browser-based generator for pixel shapes (2D) and voxel shapes (3D) rendered with Minecraft block textures. Pick a block, dial in a size, get a PNG. No installation, no account, no backend.

2. Modes & shapes

2D · Circle / Ellipse

Circle (single Size) or Ellipse (Width + Height) tiled with the chosen block texture.

3D · Sphere / Ellipsoid

Voxel sphere (single Size) or Ellipsoid (W + H + D). Each visible voxel is textured.

Cut (3D)

Slice along the X, Y or ⟋ (45° diagonal) axis. Switching axis restores the full figure — only one cuts at a time. Slider max scales to the chosen axis.

3. Algorithms (2D)

Euclidean

Distance test at pixel centres. Smoothest contour.

Bresenham

Classic midpoint algorithm. Stair-stepped pixel-art look.

Threshold

Corner-coverage. Chunkier silhouette at the same size.

4. Controls

Mode & shape

2D / 3D and Circle / Ellipse (Sphere / Ellipsoid in 3D) toggles at the top.

Block picker

Click any block tile in the picker strip below the canvas. Random mixes natural blocks across cells. Arrow keys walk the picker after selection.

Pinch & rotate

Two fingers zoom; in 3D the midpoint also rotates. Mouse wheel zooms; in 3D click-drag rotates. Double-click resets the camera (3D) or zoom (2D).

Grid & wireframe

The grid corner button toggles a cell grid in 2D and a per-voxel edge overlay in 3D. Default is OFF for transparent blocks (Glass / Ice) and ON for everything else — preferences are kept separately.

Sound & theme

The speaker button in the topbar mutes all sounds (placement clicks, fuse, easter-egg samples). The sun / moon button toggles a night mood that dims background panels only — text and block tiles stay bright.

Undo / Redo

Ctrl+Z undoes the last figure-changing edit; any of Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Alt+Z redoes. Visual-only toggles (camera, edges, theme, sound) are not tracked.

Exports

The top-right corner has PNG (current canvas) and .schem (Sponge Schematic v2, gzipped NBT) — the latter loads in WorldEdit, Litematica and MCEdit.

Easter eggs

An oak tree grows on top of the figure when any size slider hits 15 with Grass Block / Dirt / Random selected. A creeper stands on top instead when the slider is at 15 with TNT selected — and slowly turns to lock eyes with the camera every ~16 s, glowing primed-white as it fires the TNT fuse.

Keyboard
