Math companion

A Matemática do Projeto — full technical companion in nine languages. Covers the implicit ellipse and ellipsoid equations, three rasterization algorithms (Euclidean, Bresenham, threshold) with derivations, voxel volume, planar cuts including the 45° diagonal, spherical-coordinate camera, auto-zoom by bounding sphere, Lambertian shading approximation, plus five Minecraft-specific extensions: block-texture UV mapping, inventory arithmetic (packs of 64 and double chests of 3,456 items), highlight-overlay exposed-face counting, layer-by-layer horizontal decomposition, and octahedral-symmetry construction-cost reduction via /clone.
