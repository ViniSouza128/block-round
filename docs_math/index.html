A full math companion in nine languages and a Brazilian classroom lesson plan. Covers the implicit ellipse and ellipsoid equations, three 2D rasterization algorithms (Euclidean, Bresenham, threshold), voxelization, planar cuts (including the 45° diagonal), Lambertian shading and five Minecraft-specific extensions: inventory packs of 64 with double-chest arithmetic, exposed-face counting, layer-by-layer construction, octahedral-symmetry /clone optimization, and block-texture UV mapping.

Rebuilding: cd docs_math && python build.py (requires XeLaTeX/MiKTeX with unicode-math, polyglossia, xeCJK, tcolorbox, needspace, tabularx, xurl).

Each math PDF is ≥25 pages and embeds 8 figures from img/ plus a full bibliography in the local language; mathematical content is identical across locales — only the prose and bibliography comments change.

The lesson plan PT-BR uses 16 figures captured from the app, full BNCC alignment (EM13MAT307/308/309/404), and includes five regional adaptations (urbanas / interior / IFs / campo / indígenas-quilombolas) plus a Litematica build tutorial for in-game construction.
